Global Core Mechanic: The Execution
Active: Phases 1, 2, and 3

This system serves as the central, unifying threat of the entire boss fight. It is not tied to a standard cooldown; instead, it acts as an immediate, highly lethal punishment that triggers automatically when a player fails specific mechanics.

The Mark (Sigil): Players are afflicted with the Sigil when they get hit by specific hazard attacks throughout the fight (e.g., the final blade of Volley, the sweeping lasers of Chord and Glissando, or any of the uncounterable Phase 3 tacticals).

The Trigger (Requiem): The exact moment any player receives the Sigil, Astraea instantly cancels her current animation or attack. She channels briefly, drawing glowing lines connecting her to all marked targets in the arena, and unleashes a blindingly fast, linear chain-dash directly through each of them.

The Counterplay (Dash Parry): Fleeing from Requiem is impossible. The targeted player must stand their ground, face Astraea head-on, and execute a perfect Parry at the exact moment of collision.

The Resolution:

If Parried: Astraea's momentum is violently halted. She suffers a massive poise break and enters a prolonged stagger state, creating a huge damage window for the team. All Sigils are instantly cleansed from the party.

If Failed: The marked player takes massive execution damage (e.g., 50% Max HP + heavy flat damage).

Phase 1: The Virtuoso
Theme: A calculated introduction to Astraea's rhythm. This phase focuses on ranged zoning, basic parrying, and teaching players the deadly consequence of her global execution mechanic.

The Skillset:

Waltz (Basic Melee): A sequenced, multi-step melee combo. This acts as the fundamental rhythm check, teaching players standard parry timings.

Volley (Basic Ranged): Astraea throws a sequence of blades from a distance. The final blade in the sequence glows—if it hits a player, it applies the Sigil.

Chord (Tactical): Astraea calculates a linear trajectory to intersect the highest number of players, then violently throws two blades to the start and end of that line. A static, crackling beam of energy zaps between the two grounded blades. Anyone caught in the line takes damage and receives the Sigil.

Phase 2: The Maestro
Theme: Astraea shifts her focus from basic rhythm to heavy, punishing engagements and dynamic spatial control. This phase tests the players' patience, discipline, and spatial awareness as the arena becomes actively hostile.

The Skillset:

Crescendo (Basic Melee): A devastating, slow-winding overhead slam designed to test discipline. It features a long Red Frame wind-up (which violently punishes players who spam attacks early) followed by a brief Green Frame right before impact. Players must patiently wait and strike during the Green Frame to execute a Normal Counter, canceling the attack and staggering her.

Glissando (Tactical): A deadly escalation of Phase 1's Chord. Astraea throws two blades to establish the energy beam, but instead of remaining static, the blades immediately begin to slide and spin across the arena floor. This drags the beam in a massive, sweeping arc, forcing players to jump or dash through the moving laser. Anyone clipped by the sweeping beam takes damage and receives the Sigil.

Phase 3: The Blade-Dancer
Theme: The climax of the fight. Astraea abandons the formal rules of the duel and moves at blinding speeds. Her tactical skills become entirely uncounterable, bypassing shields and parries to test pure survival, spacing, and dodge timing.

The Skillset:

Eclipse (Basic Melee): Astraea vanishes into a flash of starlight and a shadow appears directly in front of a targeted player, accompanied by a distinct chime. Exactly one second later, she materializes and delivers a massive, rhythmic downward cleave. This tests a deliberate "1-2" rhythm for a perfect parry. Panic-blocking instead of parrying deals chip damage and heavy knockback, ruining the player's positioning.

Horizon (Tactical): Astraea plunges her swords into the center of the arena, releasing a massive, accelerating shockwave of starlight across the floor. It is unblockable and moves too fast at the arena's edge to outrun; players must time a jump to clear it.

Zenith (Tactical): Swords materialize directly above all living players. They track movement perfectly for a brief window before plummeting down in a staggered, random order. Unblockable; forces every player to watch their own blade and dodge-roll away at the exact frame it drops.

Gale (Tactical): Astraea summons a halo of 17 blades that aggressively target the furthest players in the room. They tilt to show their tracking paths, hover tensely, and then fire all at once. Unblockable; players must use rapid movement (sprinting/strafing) to outmaneuver the swarm.
